Manga Personal — Privacy Policy

Developer: ReyGab • Effective: November 2026 • Package: io.github.reygab.manga_personal • Contact: reygab.support@gmail.com

1. Local Data Stored on Your Device

  • Saved web bookmarks, chapter titles, reading progress, and reading history are stored locally on your device in SQLite (Drift).
  • Theme, Pure-Black AMOLED preferences, reader defaults, browser safety preferences, and in-app purchase entitlements are stored locally in SharedPreferences.
  • Manga Personal does not require account registration and does not operate a remote user-data server.

2. Built-In WebView Browser & Cookies

  • Webpages load directly from user-entered URLs or search queries. No copyrighted or third-party comic catalogs are preloaded.
  • Search queries entered in the browser are sent over HTTPS directly to Google Search (google.com).
  • SafeSearch and Pop-up & Redirect Guard are enabled by default and configurable inside Settings and the Browser menu.
  • Incognito tabs do not save search queries to Recent Searches or persistent history.
  • You can clear browser cache, cookies, and recent searches anytime in Settings → Clear Browser Cache & Cookies or the Browser menu.

3. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob + UMP)

  • Adaptive banner ads appear only on the native Library and History screens.
  • Zero ads are ever displayed inside the built-in WebView browser or inside ReaderScreen.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may collect and process the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) / device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction and performance data, and crash/diagnostic telemetry for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Where required (such as EEA/UK), Google UMP consent controls allow managing ad privacy preferences directly inside the app. Banner ads are hidden permanently when remove_ads or supporter_pro is purchased, or whenever ad consent is withdrawn.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • Optional one-time non-consumable upgrades (remove_ads and supporter_pro) are processed securely by Google Play Billing.
  • Manga Personal never accesses or stores payment card or financial credentials.

5. Your Controls & Data Deletion

  • Clear reading history, clear browser cache & cookies, or remove bookmarks anytime within the app.
  • Uninstalling Manga Personal permanently removes all locally stored SQLite and preference data from your device.

6. Third-Party Search, Image Proxy, Network Metadata & Contact

  • Search queries entered in the browser are sent over HTTPS directly to Google Search (google.com), and visited sites receive standard network metadata (IP address, User-Agent, Referer).
  • When rendering web cover thumbnails or comic pages encoded in .avif (or as a fallback when a direct image fails to decode on-device), Manga Personal requests a WebP-transcoded copy over HTTPS via https://wsrv.nl/. wsrv.nl receives the requested public image URL and standard HTTPS request metadata (device IP address); site cookies and Referer headers from your browsing session are not forwarded to wsrv.nl.
  • For privacy inquiries, data questions, or support, contact developer ReyGab at reygab.support@gmail.com.
